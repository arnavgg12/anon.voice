Questions

Do I need an account?

No. You're a guest the moment you press start — no email, no password, no profile.

Is it really anonymous?

Yes. You're never asked for your name. Voice and game data go directly between you and your partner (peer-to-peer); the server only helps you find each other.

Is anything recorded?

No. There's no recording or chat history. When the chat ends, it's gone for good.

Do I need a webcam?

Never. This is voice and text only — no video, ever.

What if someone's rude?

Hit Skip to instantly move on, or Report to flag them and move on at the same time.

How many can be in a room?

Public rooms hold up to 5 people so the audio stays clear.
